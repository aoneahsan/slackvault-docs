There's no migration here. Pumble is a hosted chat you'd move your team to, and SlackVault has no import from it. If your integrations and partners are in Slack, SlackVault copies your messages out and leaves the team where it is.

## Where to start

[Slack app setup](/guides/slack-app-setup), then [Firebase setup](/guides/firebase-setup), then the [browser extension](/guides/browser-extension). The first sync is covered in [Syncing & your archive](/guides/syncing-and-archive).
