Pumble is hosted team chat whose free plan states unlimited users and unlimited message history, with nothing to run yourself. Getting that means switching the team to Pumble.

SlackVault suits a team whose integrations, habits and partners are in Slack, where the goal is to keep that history safe rather than move everyone to another app. It has no import from Pumble.

## Where to start

Set up the [Slack app](/guides/slack-app-setup), point it at a Firebase project ([Firebase setup](/guides/firebase-setup)) and load the [browser extension](/guides/browser-extension); the first sync is in [Syncing & your archive](/guides/syncing-and-archive).

Pumble's pages did not state whether it saves Slack's public or private channels, DMs or threads, runs on a schedule, keeps the copy in storage you control, exports to open formats, works without admin rights, or puts records back into Slack. Those rows are left out.
