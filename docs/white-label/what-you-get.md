# What a white-label licence delivers

## The web group

The one group offered today. It covers:

- the SlackVault website and archive dashboard, deployed on your domain;
- your name, mark and colours on the site, the dashboard and the emails it sends;
- the OAuth exchange, run with your own Slack app's credentials;
- every SlackVault update deployed to your copy for the included months, then for as long as you renew.

## What is not offered yet

| Group | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Android + iOS app | The Android app builds, but it is not released on Google Play. |
| Browser extension | It builds, but it is not in the Chrome, Edge or Firefox stores. |

Syncing runs in the SlackVault browser extension, which is not part of a web licence. A branded extension is
[custom work](https://slackvault.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

## What stays with each team

- **Their Firebase project.** Messages, channels and members are written there. See
  [Firebase setup](../guides/firebase-setup.md).
- **File copies**, when switched on, go to our file storage at a public, hard-to-guess link, as they do on
  SlackVault itself.

## What stays with you

- **Your Slack app registration.** Your copy talks to Slack through a Slack app registered in your own Slack
  account, under your name. We set it up with you, following [Slack app setup](../guides/slack-app-setup.md).
  It is not listed in the Slack Marketplace, and we do not promise a listing.
- **Your domain** and any paid service you choose, and their costs.
