# Updates and renewals

An update is a fix or feature we ship to SlackVault itself, deployed to your copy. Release 0.2.0, for example,
added thread replies and imports from official Slack exports.

- **Included:** six months of updates from the day you buy, with a licence or a buyout.
- **Renewals:** 3, 6 or 12 months at a time, bought with your licence key on the
  [updates page](https://slackvault.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates), which lists the current price of each.
- **How a renewal counts:** from the later of today and the day your current updates end, so renewing early loses
  nothing.
- **Without a renewal:** your copy keeps running on the last version we deployed.

A change made only for your copy is not an update; it is [custom work](https://slackvault.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).
