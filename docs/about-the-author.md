## About SlackVault

SlackVault grew out of a simple idea: your Slack history is *your* data, and keeping a portable,
searchable copy of it shouldn't cost money or hand control to a vendor. The result is a zero-cost
platform where the browser does the work and your archive lives in your own Firebase project.

- **Product / web app:** [slackvault.aoneahsan.com](https://slackvault.aoneahsan.com)
- **Android app:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aoneahsan.slackvault)
- **Documentation:** you're reading it.
